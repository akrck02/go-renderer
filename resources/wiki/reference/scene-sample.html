scene/sample

import "github.com/akrck02/go-renderer/scene/sample"

Package sample builds a small made-up island that uses every feature the renderers draw: relief with snow, the sea with a tide, a river, instanced trees with levels of detail that sway, a house with stone, shingle and plank patterns, a paved square, a glowing lamp, a waterfall, an unlit flag, a glass box, the sky with clouds, moon and a constellation, and a label. It needs no files, so tests and backend comparisons can draw the same scene anywhere.
