Lesson 3: light, sky and water

So far the island floats in grey. In this lesson you fill scene.Environment: the sea around it, fog in the distance, wind in the trees, clouds, and the night, with a lamp that glows in it.

Mission Pieces Time
The sea, the wind, clouds and the night scene.Environment, Water, Sky, KindGlow 15 min

The idea

The scene’s weather report: one struct says where the sun is, how far the fog reaches, how high the sea comes and which way the wind blows. There is no clock in it: the sun’s height is the time of day, as a sundial reads it.

The environment

Everything that is not a node lives in world.Environment: the sun, the sky, fog, water, wind, snow and the season’s tint on leaves. scene.New() starts from scene.DefaultEnvironment(), a clear afternoon; you change only what you need.

The sea

Water is an endless animated plane at a height. It is coloured by how deep the water is over the ground below it, so the shore shines turquoise and the open sea turns dark blue on its own:

world.Environment.Water = &scene.Water{
	Level:   1,    // one metre above zero: the hill's edges go under
	Size:    2000, // how far the plane reaches
	Shallow: graphics.Vec4{0.1, 0.5, 0.55, 1},
	Deep:    graphics.Vec4{0.02, 0.12, 0.25, 1},
}

Fog and wind

world.Environment.FogColor = graphics.Vec4{0.75, 0.8, 0.85, 1}
world.Environment.FogNear, world.Environment.FogFar = 80, 400 // FogFar 0 means no fog
world.Environment.Wind = scene.Wind{Direction: graphics.Vec4{1, 0, 0.3, 0}, Strength: 0.5}

The wind moves the clouds and bends every material with a Sway. Plants are modelled with their base at the origin and a height of one; the higher a vertex, the more it bends:

forest.Mesh.Material.Sway = 0.3

Clouds

scene.Sky describes the sky over a whole day: stars, a moon with phases, clouds, constellations, or pictures (a skybox). Start from Earth’s:

sky := scene.DefaultSky()
sky.Clouds = scene.Clouds{Coverage: 0.4, Color: graphics.Vec4{1, 1, 1, 1}, Speed: 0.01, Scale: 1}
world.Environment.Sky = sky

Day and night

There is no clock in the renderer: the sun’s height is the time of day. Above the horizon it is day, near it twilight, below it night, when the moon lights the scene and the stars turn about the pole. Environment.SunDirection points towards the sun; the viewer’s keys I K J L move it and N jumps between day and night, and the flags -night, -sun-elevation and -sun-azimuth start there.

A lighthouse should shine at night. A glowing material is lit like any other by day and shows its own colour in the dark:

lamp.Mesh.Material.Kind = scene.KindGlow

go run . -night

Material kinds

Kind Shading
KindLit Sun or moon, sky light, shadows, fog: most things
KindUnlit Its colour as it is (plus fog): flags, markers, things that should not darken
KindGlow Lit by day, shining in the dark: lamps, windows, glowing flowers
KindWater An animated water surface: a river or a lake above the sea’s level
KindWaterfall Translucent falling water

Checkpoint

The hill rises out of a sea, turquoise at the shore and dark further out; the trees sway; with -night, the lamp glows yellow over the dark island.

What you learnt

  • Environment holds the sun, the sky, fog, water, wind and snow.
  • The sun’s height is the time of day; the night comes with the moon and the stars.
  • Sway bends plants in the wind; KindGlow shines at night.

Bonus (optional)

Give the island a winter: snow above two and a half metres, fading in over half a metre, and foliage turned a little brown.
